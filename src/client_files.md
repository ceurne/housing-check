# How to use these files

Every group calculates all eight clients with their own model. Each group then writes the full advice in English for one client only, assigned by the teacher.

All eight clients are international newcomers moving to Noord-Holland for work. They write in English and expect advice in English. None of them has chosen a home yet: they describe their wishes, and the students search the housing database (240 homes) for homes that fit.

For some clients, a home exists that meets every wish. Other clients will have to give up one wish. Students do not know in advance which situation applies.

Each e-mail also contains details that do not matter for the model, and some that do matter in real life but are left out of the model (a car lease, a temporary contract). Students decide what goes into the model and what they mention as a risk in their advice.

# Client 1: Mateus and Joana Silva

**From:** Mateus Silva
**Subject:** Looking for a family home in Haarlem

Dear advisor,

My wife Joana and I are moving from Porto to the Netherlands in January, together with our two children (6 and 9) and our very energetic dog, Biscoito. We would like to buy a home, and a colleague of Joana gave us your name.

Joana has a permanent contract as a nurse at a hospital in Haarlem. I work remotely as a software tester for a company in Lisbon. My salary is paid in Portugal, but it is stable. Together we earn **€95,000 gross per year**. We have saved **€40,000** that we can use for the purchase.

These are our wishes:

- We want to live **in Haarlem**, because of Joana's job and the international school.
- We need **at least three bedrooms**.
- We want a **garden** for the children and the dog.
- We would like to be **no more than 2 km from a train station**, because I travel to Amsterdam once a week.

We know Haarlem is expensive. If we cannot find a home that has everything, please tell us which wish we should give up.

Some more about us: we lease a car (a Volkswagen Golf) for €280 per month, which we will keep. Joana loves gardening and I play futsal twice a week. The children are already learning a few Dutch words.

Our questions are: how much can we spend, which homes are realistic for us, and what should we think about before we buy?

Thank you for your help!
Mateus
